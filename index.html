About Our Business

Building value in the paddy rice supply chain.

Adamu Hassan Paddy Rice Enterprise is a CAC-registered business operating in the paddy rice value chain. Our work focuses on connecting local farmers with customers through paddy rice purchasing, aggregation, storage and supply.


Our expansion focus is to increase aggregation, improve storage and transportation, reduce post-harvest losses and reach more customers.

Our Mission

To provide reliable access to quality paddy rice while creating better market opportunities for farmers and dependable supply for customers.
